OUR STORY

THE ESSENCE OF VISHAYU

Maison Vishayu was created around one simple belief — fragrance is more than a scent. It is identity, memory and presence.

Our collection brings together the soul of traditional Itra with a refined modern presentation, creating fragrances designed to become part of your personality.

From deep Oud to nostalgic Mitti and elegant Musk, every bottle tells a different story.
